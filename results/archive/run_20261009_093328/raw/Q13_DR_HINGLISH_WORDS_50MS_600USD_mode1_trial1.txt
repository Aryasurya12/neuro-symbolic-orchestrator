**Result:** It is not possible to satisfy **all** of the stated requirements simultaneously.

| Requirement | What the catalog shows | Feasibility |
|-------------|------------------------|-------------|
| **Disaster‑recovery across AWS + GCP** | Must pick one AWS region and one GCP region. The cheapest pair that meets the latency limit (< 50 ms) is **us‑east‑1 (AWS)** ↔ **us‑central1 (GCP)** (32 ms). | ✅ |

Because the **fixed regional base fees** alone are more than double the allowed budget, no combination of instance types, bandwidth, or replica counts can bring the total cost down to $100 while still providing the required AWS + GCP disaster‑recovery, 99.99 % (or higher) uptime, and sub‑50 ms latency.

**What can be done?**

1. **Raise the budget** – at least $250 / mo is needed just for the two regions (including latency surcharge).  
2. **Relax the disaster‑recovery scope** – use a single‑cloud DR (e.g., only AWS) or drop the latency requirement.  
3. **Accept a lower SLA** – choosing regions with lower base costs (none exist in the catalog) would be required.

Given the current catalog and constraints, a viable deployment cannot be produced.
