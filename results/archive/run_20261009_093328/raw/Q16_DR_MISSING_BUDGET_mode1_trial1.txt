### Why this mix?
* **Two clouds** – AWS (us‑east‑1) + Azure (eastus) satisfy the “across two clouds” requirement.
* **Cost‑effective DR link** – the lowest latency pair (12 ms) keeps the cross‑cloud DR surcharge to just $3.
* **Compute sizing** – a modest t3.medium for the primary workload and a single Azure D4s_v5 node for DR (you can replace it with a smaller Azure SKU if you need to cut cost further).
* **Dynamic scaling** – 200 Mbps bandwidth with 3 worker replicas gives ~89 % CPU utilisation per the model, providing headroom while keeping scaling costs low.
* **High combined SLA** – 99.999975 % availability, well above typical 99.9 % enterprise targets.

Feel free to adjust bandwidth or replica count if your traffic patterns change; the cost formulas scale linearly.
