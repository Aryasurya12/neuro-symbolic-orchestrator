### Why this configuration
* **Oracle‑compatible VM** – t3.large provides enough CPU & memory for a medium‑size Oracle instance while staying well within the 730 h/month budget.
* **Multi‑region DR** – us‑east‑1 (primary) + us‑west‑2 (standby) gives the lowest combined DR cost and latency (65 ms) among the AWS options, yielding a combined SLA of **99.99975 %** (1‑((1‑0.9995)²)).
* **Dynamic scaling** – 300 Mbps bandwidth covers typical DB‑sync and client traffic; 4 worker replicas give exactly 300 Mbps of processing capacity (75 Mbps per replica) and keep CPU utilization at ~100 % (the model’s ceiling), ensuring no throttling.
* **Cost‑effective** – All selected SKUs are within the catalog and the total stays under $500/mo.
