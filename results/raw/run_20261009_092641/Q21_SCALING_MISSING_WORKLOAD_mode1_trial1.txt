**Provider & Regions**  
- **AWS – us‑east‑1** (base $120 /mo)  
- **GCP – us‑central1** (base $115 /mo)  

These two regions give the lowest disaster‑recovery (DR) cost:

\[
\text{DR cost}=120+115+(32\text{ ms}\times0.25)=\boxed{\$243.00\;/\text{mo}}
\]

**Continuous‑Dynamic‑Scaling Configuration**

| Parameter | Value | Cost |
|-----------|-------|------|
| Target CPU utilisation | **70 %** (by design) |
| Replicas (workers) | **16** (max allowed) |
| Bandwidth | **840 Mbps** (16 × 52.5 Mbps) |
| Bandwidth cost | 840 Mbps × $0.08 = **$67.20** |
| Replica cost | 16 × $45 = **$720.00** |
| **Scaling total** | – | **$787.20** |

*Derivation*:  
CPU = (Bandwidth / (Replicas × 75 Mbps)) × 100 % → to hit 70 % → Bandwidth = 0.70 × Replicas × 75 = 52.5 × Replicas.  
Choosing the maximum allowed replicas (16) keeps Bandwidth = 840 Mbps ≤ the 1000 Mbps ceiling and yields the highest throughput while staying well under the $1500 budget.

**Overall Monthly Cost**

\[
\text{Total} = \text{Scaling cost} + \text{DR cost}
= 787.20 + 243.00 = \boxed{\$1,030.20\;/\text{month}}
\]

**Recommendation Summary**

- **Provider:** AWS (us‑east‑1) + GCP (us‑central1) for DR.  
- **Instance types:** No dedicated VM cost is needed beyond the scaling model; the workers run on the 16 × replica pool.  
- **Bandwidth:** 840 Mbps (cost $67.20).  
- **Worker replicas:** 16 (cost $720.00).  
- **DR pair:** us‑east‑1 ↔ us‑central1 (cost $243.00).  

**Result:** A continuously scaling service that runs at ~70 % CPU, delivers 840 Mbps of throughput, and stays well within the $1500 budget at a total monthly expense of **$1,030.20**.
