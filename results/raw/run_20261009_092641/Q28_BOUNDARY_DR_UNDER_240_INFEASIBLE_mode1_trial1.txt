**Result – the budget cannot be met exactly.**

### Recommendation
- **Use the us‑east‑1 ↔ us‑central1 pair** – it meets the latency (32 ms < 50 ms) and far exceeds the SLA requirement (99.999975 % > 99.99 %).
- Accept a slight budget overrun of $3/month, or increase the monthly budget to at least **$243** to stay within the technical constraints.
