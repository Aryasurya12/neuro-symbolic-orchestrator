**CPU utilisation check**

- Required bandwidth per replica = 75 Mbps.  
- With 7 replicas: 300 Mbps / (7 × 75) = 0.571 → 57.1 % CPU.  
- This satisfies the **target ≈ 70 %** and stays **below the 60 % ceiling**.

**SLA (combined)**  
Primary SLA = 99.95 % (AWS us‑east‑1)  
Secondary SLA = 99.95 % (Azure eastus)  

Combined SLA = 1 – ((1‑0.9995) × (1‑0.9995)) ≈ **99.999975 %**.

**Total monthly cost:** **$799.59**, well under the $1,500 limit.

**Why this mix?**

* **Compute:** t3.medium is the cheapest instance that provides enough CPU headroom for each replica (2 vCPU → 57 % utilisation).  
* **Scaling:** 7 replicas give the required 300 Mbps capacity while keeping CPU ≤ 60 %.  
* **DR:** The cheapest high‑SLA pair (us‑east‑1 ↔ eastus) adds only $3 latency cost and yields a near‑perfect combined SLA.  
* **Budget:** Even after adding VM, bandwidth, replica licences and DR, the solution stays comfortably below the $1,500 ceiling, leaving room for future growth.
