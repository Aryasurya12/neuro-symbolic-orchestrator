**Provider:** AWS  

**Region:** us‑east‑1 (cheapest AWS region in the catalog)  

**Compute:** 2 × c5.xlarge  
- vCPU = 4 × 2 = 8 vCPU  
- RAM = 8 GB × 2 = 16 GB  

**Dynamic‑Scaling (optional, fits the budget):**  
- Bandwidth = 200 Mbps → 200 × $0.08 = $16.00 /mo  
- Worker replicas = 2 (75 Mbps per replica → 2 × 75 = 150 Mbps < 200 Mbps, so we add a third replica to meet capacity)  
  → 3 replicas × $45 = $135.00 /mo
