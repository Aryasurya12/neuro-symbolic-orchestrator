Natural language cloud requests, solved exactly.

neurasym parses natural language and colloquial requests into formal constraint models, matches them to pre-compiled symbolic optimization templates (ILP, Continuous PSO, and Z3 SMT), and proves global cost optimality with zero constraint violations.

How it works

The four sequential stages of the neuro-symbolic pipeline.

STEP 01
Parse
Disentangles natural language and colloquial terms into structured resource parameters and budget constraints.
STEP 02
Match
Computes Jaccard similarity across the CARM archetype index to select the sound solver template.
STEP 03
Solve
Executes exact mathematical optimization (MILP, Continuous PSO, Z3 SMT) with hard constraint verification.
STEP 04
Explain
Translates solver proofs, slack variables, and costs into deterministic plain English summaries.
